Challenge 3

A review queue built for speed

Work through the ranked applicants one at a time. Use the buttons or the keys S, H and R. Rejecting asks for a reason, which keeps decisions consistent and auditable.

  • The reasons for the rank sit next to the decision.
  • Undo is one key away.
